`session-observed` rows are kept separate on purpose, and the table
shows why: on Windows they are FASTER than the controlled
`single-file` runs, which cannot be true of a real edit-and-rebuild.

The cause is the command that produced them. It ran the build twice --
once piped to `grep error:`, once piped to `tail -1` for the time --
so the number reported came from the SECOND run, with nothing left to
do. Those rows are mostly no-op builds wearing an edit-build label.

Kept rather than deleted, because a plausible wrong number is worth
recognising: it looked like a normal measurement for a whole session.
Do not mix them into a planning figure.

`session-observed` 的資料列刻意分開，而上表正好顯示了原因：在 Windows 上，它們竟比受控的
`single-file` 更快，而這對於真正的「改一下、再建一次」是不可能的。

原因出在產生它們的那道指令：它把建置跑了兩次——一次導向 `grep error:`、一次導向
`tail -1` 以取得時間——因此被記下的數字來自**第二次**執行，而那時已無事可做。那些資料列
多半是掛著「編輯後建置」標籤的 no-op 建置。

此處保留而非刪除，因為「看似合理的錯誤數字」值得被認得出來：它在整個工作階段中，看起來
都像是一次正常的量測。請勿把它們併入用於規劃的數字。
